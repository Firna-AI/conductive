`app/orgs/new/operator` has Create org of an operator, from e2b decision 222.
An e2b decision is a decision of the change that adds E2B as the second sandbox provider.
Provider account under the repositories offers By weights, the default,
`boat`, and `e2b`, and its hint says that only operators see it. Its `e2b`
variant has `e2b` chosen for Acme Labs, a test org, and its `first`
variant has the first org of an operator in Get started. Create org in the
rail and in the org switcher of an operator opens it. `app/orgs/new` and
its other child pages show no choice, because nobody else sees it.

`app/new-workspace/dialog` draws New workspace as a dialog over the ready
workspace of `app`, for a new account or a user who chose the dialog.
Its states are child pages. `first-build` shows Firna before its first
good build, with the notice and Create off. `section` shows the open menu of the sections at
the top right. The other child pages show files that upload, a
drop of files on the whole dialog card, a draft with uploaded files and
the notice of a missing agent login, a

pasted workspace link as a chip. The dialog uses the file chips of
`app/new-workspace/files`. A phone always opens the page, so each phone

`user-flows/create-workspace-dialog` goes from Preferences through the
dialog to the new workspace. Its child page `org-login` has a draft on the

The `viewer` control of `app/new-workspace/dialog` sets one viewer for the

`app/new-workspace/dialog/org-login` has the dialog of Priya over the ready

`app/archive` draws Archive and Restore of Checkout address autocomplete,
from decisions 1 and 2 of PR #138, and the removal of an archived workspace

- `app/archive/just-archived` has the workspace just after Archive from the
  pull request bar, with the toast, the day when the workspace leaves the

- `app/archive/group` has the list with the Archived group open, and

- `app/archive/restored` has the workspace back in its section, stopped,

- `app/archive/pending` has the archived workspace while an unresolved

their folder of [`src/ui/components/`](../../src/ui/components/) or of
`src/components/`, and `components/screens` for the registered helper

registration has no page there. Each part has its own page and at most four
variants, such as `components/workspaces/workspace-mark`.
