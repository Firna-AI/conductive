# Decision numbers collide across parallel plans

- **Severity:** Low. **Effort:** Small. **Confidence:** High. **Harder later:** No.
- **Kind:** Needs decision.
- **Added:** 2026-11-09, from the options check of a decision of the plan `plans/automatic-first-build.md`, branch `calummoore/box-auto-build-onboarding`. On 2026-11-09 it also took in an item of `calummoore/workspace-link-widget-brainstorm` about the repeats in the live docs.
- **Location:** the section "Decision numbers" of `docs/architecture.md`, `plans/`, and the decision citations in `docs/protocol/`.
- **Reason:** the numbering rule is a repository rule for every plan, outside the scope of the change that found the collision.

- **Source:** Review. **Category:** Process.
- **Terms:**
  - **Decision number:** the number of one dated decision record, such as "decision 36". Protocol docs, mockups, and code comments cite it.
- **Background:**
  - `docs/architecture.md` says that a decision number names the dated record of one decision, and that the plan of the change keeps the records while the change is open.
  - The pull request of the change keeps the records after the merge.
  - Most decisions live in `plans/boat-native-remote-workspaces.md`, which numbers them in order.
  - The plan `plans/automatic-first-build.md` took the next free numbers twice, 142 to 144 and then 147 to 149. Each time, `origin/main` used the same numbers for other decisions within hours, so each citation named two decisions.
  - The live docs already hold such repeats, as **Evidence** lists. A number alone can then lead a reader to the wrong record.
- **Problem:** Two plans that run at the same time take the same numbers, because no rule reserves them.
- **Evidence:**
  - Decision 142 names three records:
    - the operator section, in `docs/protocol/operator-section.md` and `docs/protocol/access-and-security.md`
    - the rule that the last edit that the server commits to a session field wins, in `docs/protocol/agents-and-sessions.md`
    - the Preferences tab, in `docs/protocol/create-a-workspace.md`
  - Decision 145 names three records:
    - the palette key, in `docs/protocol/command-palette.md`
    - the masked file paths, in `docs/protocol/access-and-security.md` and `src/marketing/privacy.ts`
    - the lift of a suspension, in `docs/protocol/operator-actions.md` and `docs/protocol/operator-section.md`
  - Decisions 150 and 151 each name two records:
    - the fold of the work of an ended turn, in `docs/protocol/chat-and-events.md` and `docs/protocol/agent-tools.md`
    - the first build of a new org, in `docs/protocol/org-image.md`, `docs/protocol/orgs-and-members.md`, and `docs/protocol/create-a-workspace.md`
  - The workspace link chips change of `calummoore/workspace-link-widget-brainstorm` also used 150 and 151 at first. Before its merge, it renumbered them:
    - 150 became 211, "Chat cannot point at another workspace": the chips of workspace links. The user chose option D on 9 November 2026.
    - 151 became 212, "Workspaces have no short number": the workspace numbers and their search. The user chose option D on 9 November 2026.
    - At commit `e75a4e5`, `docs/protocol/workspace-links.md` cites decision 150 four times and decision 151 twice. `docs/protocol/workspaces-and-sandboxes.md` cites 151 for the numbers.
    - At commit `dfc2723`, the same places cite 211 and 212. The choices and their dates stay the same.
- **Impact if no action:** Citations such as "decision 147" name two decisions, and each branch renumbers by hand before its merge. Agents and reviewers can then cite the wrong reason for a requirement.
- **Root question:** How does each decision get a number that no other plan uses?
- **Options:**
  - **A.** Keep the numbers in order, and renumber a plan's decisions to the next free numbers just before its pull request. Write this rule in "Decision numbers". Effort: Small.
  - **B.** Give each plan its own prefix, such as "decision auto-build-1", and keep the plain numbers of the main plan as they are. Effort: Small.
  - **C.** Reserve a block of numbers for a new plan in the main plan when the plan starts. Effort: Small.
- **Left out:** None.
- **Wider fix:** B. A prefix cannot collide, so no renumbering is needed.
- **Repair of the existing repeats:**
  - The work also repairs the repeats that **Evidence** lists, whichever option the user chooses. The repair is Medium effort, and the repair alone needs no new rule.
  - The repeats span unrelated features and are older than the workspace link chips change. So a separate branch repairs them after the merge.
  - The repair waits for the answer, because the answer chooses new numbers or prefixes.
  - Design call of 9 November 2026: give each repeated record its own number, or its own prefix with option B. Update each live reference, and keep the old number in a note beside the record.
  - Reason: distinct names meet the rule of `docs/architecture.md`, and the notes keep the old references findable.
  - A repair that adds a topic and a source link beside each repeated reference, and keeps its number, was left out. It is Medium effort, and one number then still names two records.
  - Keeping the repeats was left out. It is Small effort, but readers then tell the records apart only from the topic and the Git history.
- **Previous examples:**
  - The two renumberings of `plans/automatic-first-build.md` on 9 November 2026.
  - The renumbering of the workspace link chips decisions from 150 and 151 to 211 and 212, on 9 November 2026.
  - The live repeats of 142, 145, 150, and 151 that **Evidence** lists.
- **Recommendation:** B. It ends the collisions without a step before each merge.
- **Action:** ask the user, because the numbering is a repository rule.
